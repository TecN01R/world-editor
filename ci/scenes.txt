# The screens CI renders on the Duo simulator (ci/screenshots.sh), one a line:
#
#   name | tier | poses | variants | launch arguments | tap
#
#   tier      core: on every push that touches the app; full: with everything else, when
#             the design system or ci/ changes, or on a manual run
#   poses     closed (the outer display), open (the inner one)
#   variants  default; large (the accessibility-large text size); contrast (Increase Contrast)
#   tap       a control's label to tap before the shot, for an open menu (optional)
#
# Until every screen has a scene (`-scene <name>`), these are the old launch flags.
