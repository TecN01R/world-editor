# Where the app differs from stock iOS

Kevin, 2026-11-24: "I want to deviate as little from stock as possible, so flag
anywhere we deviate from a functionality that isn't provided by stock." This is
that list. Anything new that isn't iOS's own component or behaviour gets a line
here, with why, and is named to Kevin when it ships.

"Stock" means iOS's own component, used as it comes, in iOS's place for it.
Built from stock parts but arranged or behaving our own way still counts as a
deviation.

## Custom, and why

| What | What iOS has | Why it stays |
|---|---|---|
| **Format bar** floating above the keyboard (Bold, Italic, two lists, Open for a link) | The keyboard's accessory bar; the edit menu's Format; the "Aa" formatting panel (`UITextFormattingViewController`) | Kevin allows this one. The accessory bar counts as keyboard on the Duo: it shortens the side column and pushes toolbar items into "•••". Its actions are iOS's own (`toggleBoldface`, `toggleItalics`); lists are the page's, undoable. iOS has no public call to show its "Aa" panel from an app's button. |
| **Editing toolbar** (Kevin, 2026-11-24): while the page's own writing, title or a field has the keyboard, folded and unfolded show the same three: Back or the sidebar button, Focus, and Done; Read and More wait for the keyboard to go down | Notes keeps its bar and adds Done | His call. Built from stock toolbar items; only what shows when is ours. |
| **The edit menu's Format** holds Bold, Italic, Bulleted List and Numbered List (Kevin, 2026-11-24: "Perfect") | iOS's Format: Bold, Italic, and More… (its whole Format panel: fonts, sizes, colours, text styles, alignment) | Pages keep only these; what More… offered was lost on the next redraw. iOS's own Bold and Italic, through the text view's public edit-menu delegate; its panel, if reached another way, offers only text styles and lists. |
| **`@` mention menu** (a popover of pages as you type `@`) | No public mention or autocomplete API for text views (Notes' `>>` is private) | Nothing stock to use. Built from a stock popover and list cells. |
| **Title field and fields above the writing**, inside the page's text view | Notes uses the first line as the title | The page's name and its fields are separate from the writing by design. |
| **Empty fields hidden**, listed in Add Field's menu (one picked shows, ready to fill in) | Contacts shows only what's filled in and offers the rest in its Edit mode | Kevin, 2026-11-24: not every field of a type fits every page, and empty ones cluttered it. A page has no Edit mode, so the stock menu under the fields stands in for it. |
| **Focus mode** (one paragraph per page, sized to fit, never scrolls) | Nothing like it | Kevin's own feature (`docs/focus-mode.md`). |
| **Focus mode's paragraph dots** (`ParagraphRail`) | `UIPageControl`, vertical | Not yet swapped; the page control would bring scrubbing and VoiceOver's adjustable action. To do. |
| **Changes timeline** (a rail with a point per message) | A grouped list | Kevin: "it should be a timeline". |
| **Download bar's line under it**, centred | `ProgressView`'s value label, at the bar's left edge | Kevin, 2026-11-24: the bar's width moved with the text. |
| **Model download** (Wi-Fi only, resumes, repacks as it goes) | A background `URLSession` or Background Assets | The engine repacks the model while it streams, to avoid needing 29 GB at once; a stock download would need that done shard by shard. |
| **The conversation's layout**, as ChatGPT and Claude have it (2026-11-27, from comparing chat apps, after a large column with no bubble on each side was tried: "A little odd seeing the side of the text reversed"): the author's messages on the right in a bubble one step lighter than the background, in New York; the companion's replies on the left across the reading column (`Look.column`), at reading size; its thinking and what was filed smaller and grey; Send shown only once there's something to send | No chat view | iOS has none: stock text, arranged by the app. |
| **Swipe between reply versions**, beside the "‹ 2 of 3 ›" arrows | The arrows alone | The versions are Kevin's; the swipe is extra. Flagged; it stays unless he says. |
| **Message bar's circles** (Attach and Send) | `.buttonStyle(.glass)` and `.glassProminent` | Apple's own glass effect, sized to match the field's height; each whole circle takes taps (Kevin, 2026-11-24, dead taps beside a menu's dots). Stock button styles size themselves differently. |
| **A field's value menu** (a menu around a picker) | A menu-style `Picker` | It holds actions too ("New Place…"), which a picker can't, and keeps a long page name on one line. |
| **The drawer's title row** (the world's name, with its menu) in New York at title size, on the rows' left edge, folded | iOS's large title in the bar | iOS gives a large title no menu, and his world's name is his (New York). At a page title's size, flush with the screen's edge, it felt wrong (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "The title bar and location feel off"). |
| **Back on a page names where it goes** ("Conversation", "Pages", "Connections"), and folded, **a drawer button where Back would be** on the conversation, Connections and Pages | iOS's Back to the previous column | A page goes back to what it was opened from, not to the drawer; the drawer is a tap away, as ChatGPT's (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "an easy way to see all conversations"). |
| **The pages list as a book's contents** (Kevin, 2026-11-27, of the grouped list with its Types section: "This looks awful and not inviting or relaxing or frictionless"): a plain list, no lines between rows, the pages under their kind in the plural (Characters, Places…; untyped last, as Other Pages), each with its first line and no date; each kind's heading has a menu to edit its fields, rename it or delete it (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "shouldn't the edit character fields be also in the header label for each") | An inset grouped list of cards, with rows and counts for folders | Stock parts (a plain `List`, section headers, `listRowSeparator`, context menus); only the arrangement is ours. |
| **Recently Deleted row** that shows those pages in place | Notes' Recently Deleted folder pushes a list | Not yet tried: a stack of its own in the page's side hid its toolbar (2026-11-24). To do, carefully. |
| **Files waiting to go with a message**, as small capsules above the message bar, each with ✕ (Kevin, 2026-11-25: "pdf or text analysis") | Messages shows attachments inside its field | iOS's text field can't hold attachments; the picker itself is iOS's own (`fileImporter`), and a PDF is read by PDFKit. |
| **One companion: the world's conversation, the home screen**; a page's Back returns to it and the drawer reaches it, and a page has no companion of its own (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "I don't feel this is radical enough or frictionless enough") | A per-document assistant panel (iOS's inspector, or a sheet) | One place to talk, as in ChatGPT; it files into whichever pages what's said is about. The page's own companion (an inspector unfolded, a sheet folded) was removed. |
| **Dark only** (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "Let's stop offering a light mode I'll never use it") | Following the phone's Light or Dark setting | His call. iOS's own switch for it: Info.plist's `UIUserInterfaceStyle` set to Dark. |
| **One look, grey and white only, as ChatGPT has it**: iOS's own elevated dark greys and text colours on every screen, every control white, no accent colour (Send a white circle, links underlined, what's new a white dot), red only to delete; his words and the companion's replies in a soft off-white (`Look.ink`), as pure white glares in long reading (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "easy to read chapter upon chapter") (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "All the mixture of cool and warm colors and random icon colors just seems unprofessional and poorly thought out") | Black backgrounds, blue accent | He found the black "cold and plain"; a warm grey with an amber accent then clashed with iOS's own cool menus, keyboard and glass, and icons came out amber in some places and white in others. iOS's own colours (`systemBackground` at the elevated level, `label`), set through a navigation container's background, the window's background and tint, and `tint(.primary)`. |
| **The drawer shows for an instant at launch**, then the conversation takes its place without a slide | The app opens straight on the conversation | Its rows grew and wobbled into place the first time they slid in (Kevin, 2026-11-27): iOS sized them for the first time inside the slide. Laid out at launch instead (`RootView.settleList`). |
| **A page opens to be read, as a wiki entry** (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "I do love reading a good wiki", but not keeping one): the editor's page with typing off (docs/design.md: "Read and Edit are the same page"), its title, kind, facts and writing where the editor has them, the names of the world's other pages linked, and "Mentioned In" quoting the sentences elsewhere that name it; Edit opens the editor, Read goes back | Notes opens every note ready to type | Pages are where he reads his world; the conversation is where he writes (the conversation first). A new page opens in the editor. Stock text, grid and buttons; the writing in iOS's own text view with typing off (not SwiftUI text), so its lines break exactly as the editor's; links through the text view's own delegate and iOS's `openURL`. |
| **Punctuation as print sets it, on a page read** (`Typeset`): curly quotes and apostrophes, true dashes and ellipses; what he typed is stored as he typed it (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "the text has to look gorgeous like it was printed") | Smart Punctuation, which changes the text as it's typed, and only while the keyboard setting is on | Shown only, so his writing is never changed, whatever the keyboard did. |
| **Connections**: his world as cards on a scrollable canvas, lines between pages that point to each other, faint dashed cards for names only mentioned, new ones glowing white and growing in (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "I want to feel like my world is growing itself and I'm uncovering it"; not a map: "I'd have to know the geology and geography of the world and I don't yet") | Nothing like it | Outer Wilds' ship log, which maps discoveries "by how they point to each other". Stock buttons on a SwiftUI canvas; Reduce Motion shows new cards without growing them. |
| **A white dot on a page's row** while the companion has made or added to it and he hasn't opened it; **page names underlined** in the world conversation's filing lines, tappable | No mark for what's new in a list | Watching his world grow is the pull to come back (Kevin: "Yes, that"), with no counts, streaks or badges, which the research found make apps stressful. |
| **One reading column and one margin** (docs/design.md): the page read and edited, and the conversation with its message bar, at most 33 em of the page font, about 66 characters a line, with 20 pt at the sides (`Look.column`, `Look.margin`); the page's writing lightly hyphenated | iOS's readable content guide; text views that don't hyphenate | One measure everywhere, set by the page font, so replies don't run across the unfolded screen and a long read stays comfortable (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "easy to read chapter upon chapter of text with ease"). |
| **The screen's one main action pinned at the trailing end, as an icon** (New Conversation, New Page, Edit, Read), everything else in one ⋯ More menu; the leading end only for getting around (docs/design.md) | Primary actions, which iOS orders and folds into "•••" as room allows | The same kind of button in the same place on every screen (Kevin, 2026-11-27: "different headers body size button size button placement etc."). iOS's own pinned placement and menu. |
| **Pasting and dropping as plain text** | Rich paste | Other apps' fonts and colours (black text, unreadable in dark mode) never get in. Through iOS's paste delegate. |

## Made stock on 2026-11-24 (the audit)

- The split view: iOS lays it out; the app no longer moves the pages list while the Duo
  rotates, folds or unfolds.
- Back and the sidebar button never disappear while typing.
- Bold and Italic through iOS's own commands (with Undo).
- Paste and drop through iOS's paste delegate.
- A link's own long-press menu has Open.
- The model and version as the companion menu's title, not a disabled button.
